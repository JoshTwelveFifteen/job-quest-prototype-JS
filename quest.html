Level up your job search

A step-by-step game that helps you build the knowledge, skills and confidence to get a job, and find the career that suits you best.

Start the quest

What is the quest?

The Job Quest turns getting a job into a journey you can see. You travel through 4 worlds, from getting to know yourself to your first interviews. Each world is made of short quests. Complete them to earn XP and trophies, unlock the next world and see how far you have come.

You choose your pace. You can go back to any quest at any time, and you are never on your own.

What you will build

Knowledge

About yourself, the careers out there and how the job market works. Understand what a job spec is really asking for.

Skills

Writing a CV, searching and applying for jobs, tests and interviews. Your scores improve as you practise.

Work readiness

What employers expect, from being on time and what to wear to talking about yourself with confidence.

Find the career that is best for you

Not sure what you want to do? That is where most people start. In the first world you will find your personality type, map your skills and interests, and get career suggestions that fit you. Deep dives show what each job is really like and the steps to get there.
